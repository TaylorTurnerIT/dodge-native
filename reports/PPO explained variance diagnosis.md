# Falling explained variance leaves training verdict open

No evidence shows that more training necessarily worsens the Dodge teacher's critic, and none shows it is harmless. Explained variance (EV) fell from a first-20-update mean of **0.597973** to a last-20 mean of **0.317240**. Over the same run, entropy fell from 1.880125 to 1.745426, and the KL threshold stopped epochs early in 230 of 384 updates. Smoothed development performance improved, although the final checkpoint ended below the best one. The best checkpoint scored survival **Q1=648.25, Q4=4508.8125, mean 2240 frames** and score 3.5 / 46.59375 / 20.59375, on 64 fixed development seeds and chosen best-of-24, so it is not a held-out result. The decline warrants direct critic diagnostics before a long run. Section 4 recommends one.

## EV scores rollout-time predictions against frozen targets

EV is 1 − Var(target − prediction) / Var(target) ([SB3 utils](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DLR-RM/stable-baselines3/master/stable_baselines3/common/utils.py)). SB3 and CleanRL both compute it from values recorded during the rollout, not from post-update predictions ([SB3 PPO](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DLR-RM/stable-baselines3/master/stable_baselines3/ppo/ppo.py), [CleanRL PPO](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vwxyzjn/cleanrl/master/cleanrl/ppo.py)). The frozen Dodge code does the same. `compute_gae` sets `returns = advantages + values` (line 171), and EV uses `returns` minus the rollout values (lines 547-549) ([frozen ppo.py](../learning/runs/v2/postovernight-long-D2-boundary-prepare-r14/capsule-final/python/dodge_rl/ppo.py)). The residual therefore equals the GAE advantage, up to floating-point rounding. Logging after optimization does not turn EV into a post-update fit metric. Actor and critic produced the rollout with the same pre-update weights, so "rollout-time predictions" is the accurate description, not a mismatched policy/critic version. The targets are bootstrapped from the critic itself ([GAE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438)), so EV is not a Monte Carlo fit. At fixed predictions and targets, EV ignores a constant residual bias. Do not assume minibatch advantage normalization removes critic bias from GAE, since bootstrapped targets and episode boundaries matter. Learned PPO critics can fit poorly even in successful runs ([Ilyas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02553)).

## Target shrinkage and residual growth look the same

EV drops if residual variance rises or target variance falls. The run does not log either one. Logged value loss is a sample-weighted MSE over evolving minibatch predictions and includes mean bias. EV instead uses the variance of frozen old residuals. No mapping between them is identified, so reconstructing target variance from loss and EV proves nothing, and the notes' proxy is rejected. The proxy for lost frames per decision fell about 39%, which fits fewer deaths and a smaller target variance. A growing critic residual fits the data equally well. Within this single run, EV correlates −0.77 with value loss and −0.57 with KL. Those correlations do not rank causes.

## Shared-trunk drift is plausible, not shown

Shared policy/value networks can suffer interference between objectives ([PPG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.04416); [ICLR implementation details](https://iclr-blog-track.github.io/2022/03/25/ppo-implementation-details/)). In this run the trunk gradient norm rose from 0.393 to 0.708 across 48-update bins while the critic-head norm fell from 0.0458 to 0.0339. The trunk norm mixes actor and critic gradients, and a smaller head norm does not identify actor interference or a critic bottleneck. CleanRL checks KL after each epoch ([CleanRL PPO](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vwxyzjn/cleanrl/master/cleanrl/ppo.py)), so KL is a stopping threshold, not a hard ceiling. A KL below 0.02 reported for other domains is not a Dodge threshold. The GAE weight λγ ≈ 0.945 gives roughly 18 decisions (~73 raw frames) of geometric weighting. That is a weighting, not a planning horizon, and it does not show the model cannot plan further.

## One same-batch discriminator before any long run

No continuation from failed weights is authorized, and all failed-run eligibility and identity checks stand. The notes' best-or-final continuation would be a future, separately registered experiment with permitted lineage. Research ran nothing. The single recommended step adds critic instrumentation. Retain direct target variance and the old-residual variance, mean and MSE. Recompute post-update predictions on the same frozen batch, so ΔV is measured on identical observations. Assess several batches alongside survival, with no EV pass threshold. The notes' post-update EV ≥ 0.55 rule is rejected. High in-sample post-update EV can be ordinary fitting to self-bootstrapped targets, and high post-update with low pre-update EV does not prove staleness. Out-of-sample stability would be stronger evidence. The extra computation costs time and resources.

## Conclusion

The open question is narrow: whether EV fell because targets lost variance or because old residuals grew. Survival remains the outcome. Lower EV does not establish benefit or harm from more training, and more training is not guaranteed to lower EV.
