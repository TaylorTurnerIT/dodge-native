## F2–F3 — Deterministic cumulative-prefix scheduler

Start at H=1000; stages advance by1000 through18000. Every episode starts normally, with full history. Freeze horizon and generation at reset; never force-reset on promotion. Process completions in `(update, rollout decision, slot)` order, including the entire simultaneous batch and all update rows. Only current-generation/current-target completions enter nonoverlapping100-row windows. At least90 successes passes; a failure resets the streak. Keep partial windows across updates without passing/failing them. At the end boundary, after charging this completed update once to its entry generation, promote at most once iff exposure>=10 updates, target completions>=200 and latest passing-window streak>=2. Earlier passes count before update10 only while their streak survives later completed windows. A partial window does not erase it. Entry resets generation counters, window, streak and assignment stream. Old-generation episodes complete unchanged but never count toward new mastery. At18000 record mastery without advancement.

At H=1000 all assignments are1000. Later, each100 normal-reset assignments contain exactly70 current and30 earlier horizons. Divide30 evenly; rotate extras across blocks. Canonical SHA256 sorting permutes the indexed multiset, keyed by full paired-vector hash, generation and block, with integer-index tie-break. [experiment.json](experiment.json) specifies every byte/domain/index/rotation rule; [scheduler_reference.py](scheduler_reference.py) makes it executable without RNG or runtime imports. This quota describes episode assignments, not vector occupancy or raw-frame share. Log actual frames, interval reaches/alive-through counts, resets, consumed seeds and old/new-generation exposure for every1000-frame interval.

This is cumulative-prefix practice, not isolated practice starting at1000 or2000. No frame-counter jumps, incomplete snapshots or later-start optimization. At update60 log entered, actually exposed, mastered and untested stages separately; at most six can master. Stage7000 can enter at terminal with zero training. No stage-specific60 timeout, extension, final episode drain or promise of reaching18000.
