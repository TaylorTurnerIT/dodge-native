Click a line, a dot or a list row to isolate one run, then scrub or play it. Click empty space or press Esc to see all runs. The saved trace records the killing enemy's path and only a count of the other enemies, so other enemies are not drawn; the nearest-enemy gap is shown instead. Pattern rectangles are the fixed layout (one per pattern id); they are drawn only on frames where the pattern was active, dashed while charging and solid once lethal. Patterns 13, 14, 25 and 26 appear only at high score. Moving patterns and ids with randomised layouts show their rectangles only at the death frame.
